The short version

We collect as little as we can. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The apps keep your rolls, bets and stats on your device.

This website

billybubscraps.com is a static site. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. Our host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security.

The waitlist isn’t open yet. When it opens, we’ll collect only your email address and which app you’re interested in, to send one launch email. We won’t sell it or share it for advertising.

The apps

Practice bankroll, bet history, Fair Dice stats and settings are stored on your device. If you delete the app, they’re gone.

Purchases

Premium is bought through your phone’s app store. We receive a confirmation that you own Premium, not your payment details.
