Fair Dice · How it works

Dice you can
check yourself.

“Too many sevens” is the number-one complaint about craps apps. So every roll is out in the open.

  • Secure random dice. Each die is a fair 1 to 6 from your device’s secure random generator. The tumbling animation never decides the roll.
  • Stats in plain words. Totals vs expected, sevens vs 1 in 6, and a fairness test once you’ve rolled 180 times.
  • Dice have no memory. Not your bets, your streak or the Blazing meter can change the next roll.
  • Practice only. Play chips have no cash value. Nothing to buy back, nothing to cash out.
