Play layout

Three screens. One swipe.

The board opens first, clean and uncluttered. Swipe left for Repeaters and every hop, right for the dome. Your chips, ROLL and PRESS stay pinned on all three, and a roll toast tells you what hit wherever you are.

Press it

PRESS. Or POWER PRESS.

Your Place 8 just hit. Tap PRESS 8 +$6 to add one more unit, as many taps as you like. POWER PRESS puts the whole win back on the number. Changed your mind? Undo the last tap before the roll.

Side bets

Repeaters, and every hop.

Bet a number to repeat before a seven: the 6 six times, the 2 twice. Standard pays run 40 to 90 for 1. All 21 hops sit beside them, hard hops at 30 to 1 and easy hops at 15 to 1. Every tile shows its house edge, so the long shots stay honest.

Two rule sets

Regular or Crapless.

In Crapless only a 7 wins the come-out, and 2, 3, 11 and 12 become points. The board changes to match: ten number boxes and no Don’t side. Billy shows you what that costs: Pass is 1.41% in Regular and 5.38% in Crapless.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this gambling?

No. Billy Bubs is a practice game for adults. You play with practice chips that have no cash value, can’t be bought back as money and can’t be won as prizes.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both rule sets, every bet, the Tutor, the strategy library and Fair Dice are free. Premium Custom Rules is a one-time $4.99 unlock for Bubble Craps (price may vary by region and store).

Are the dice fair?

Every die is a fair 1 to 6 from your device’s secure random generator. The Fair Dice tab shows your own results against what fair dice should do.

How is this different from a real bubble craps machine?

Same game, same layout, same bets. Bubble craps has no shooter: the machine pops the dice with air. If you want to be the shooter, try Table Craps.

When does it launch?

Soon, on iPhone and Android. The waitlist opens shortly; this page will have the store links on launch day.

Want to be the shooter?

Table Craps puts the dice in your hand: pick two, set them, hit the back wall. Pass the phone for up to six players, and chase the Fire Bet: 4 points pay 24 to 1, 5 pay 249 to 1, all 6 pay 999 to 1.
