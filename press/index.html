Fact sheet

Billy Bubs is two free craps practice apps for iPhone and Android: Bubble Craps (the air-dome machine, Regular and Crapless) and Table Craps (shooter and dice-setting practice, pass-and-play for 2–6, Fire Bet). 18+, practice chips only, no real-money play. Premium: a one-time $4.99 unlock per app.

Brand rules

Don’t recolor, stretch or re-type the wordmark. When both mascots appear, Billie stands on the left and Billy on the right, at the same scale. Never mirror either mascot.
