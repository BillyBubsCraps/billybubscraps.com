The shooter

Pick two. Set them. Throw.

Choose two of five dice from the bowl, pick a set (3V, 2V, Straight Hardway or All-Sevens), and swipe to throw. Both dice must hit the back wall. Your rolls-per-seven (RSR) is tracked against random dice. It’s practice: setting has never been proven to beat the house, and in Standard play the result is always random.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does setting the dice change the result?

Not in Standard play. Every roll comes from a secure random generator; your set, grip and throw are practice and presentation. Controlled shooting has not been proven to beat the house. A clearly labeled skill simulation is available in Premium and kept out of Fair Dice stats.

How does the Fire Bet work?

Bet it before the new shooter’s first come-out roll. Each different point the shooter makes (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) lights up. At the seven-out, 4 points pay 24 to 1, 5 pay 249 to 1, and all 6 pay 999 to 1 (paid the moment the sixth point is made). After the first come-out roll it locks until the next shooter.

How does pass-and-play work?

Up to six players share one phone, each with their own practice bankroll. Everyone bets in turn, the shooter throws, and when the shooter sevens out the dice move to the next seat. Billie cheers the handoff.

Is it really free?

Yes. Shooter practice, multiplayer, every bet, the Tutor and Fair Dice are free. Premium is a one-time $4.99 unlock for Table Craps only (price may vary by region and store).

Is this gambling?

No. Practice chips have no cash value and can’t be cashed out or won as prizes. 18+ only.
