Who can play

Billy Bubs is for adults 18 and over (or the age of majority where you live, if higher).

Practice only

Billy Bubs is a simulation for learning and fun. Practice chips have no cash value. They can’t be purchased, exchanged, cashed out or won as prizes. Doing well in the app doesn’t mean you’ll win at a real casino.

Premium

Premium is a one-time $4.99 unlock per app, bought through your app store (price may vary by region and store). Bubble Craps Premium and Table Craps Premium are sold separately. Refunds follow the store’s policy.

Fair play

Standard rolls come from a secure random generator. Any simulated-skill feature is labeled and kept out of the Fair Dice stats.

We may update these terms before and after launch. The date at the top will change when we do.
